Who am I

A bit more about me

I'm Aditya — a BCA (Semester 5) student at SVICS, under KSV University in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. I like building things that feel handmade even when they run on code: scroll stories, WebRTC calls, tiny 3D worlds.

Most of my recent hours go into front-end craft — scroll-driven animation, custom cursors, and shaking off the "generic AI look" one wobble at a time. I've also spent time deep in full-stack territory: Node, Socket.IO, WebRTC mesh calling, MongoDB, PHP.

Outside of solo projects, I show up to hackathons — most recently the Odoo Hackathon 2026 and the Meta × PyTorch OpenEnv Hackathon in Bengaluru.
